# Rights and provenance review

This repository relates to the Android APK **FOREX-MOTION-564-silver-workspaces.apk** supplied by its distributor. The archive's individual code and asset owners have **not yet been identified**.

## Important distinction

Possessing or modifying an APK does not necessarily convey the copyright or redistribution rights for every file it contains. Decompiled DEX, native libraries, licensed SDKs, images, fonts, effect presets, and shaders may be owned by other parties.

## Publication rules

- **Original source authored by this project's maintainers:** may be shared under a license chosen by its copyright holder after verifying authorship.
- **Third-party open-source code:** share only in compliance with the applicable licenses, including notice and source obligations when required.
- **Third-party proprietary code, assets, or reverse-engineered application files:** do not publish unless the rightsholder has authorized redistribution.
- **Signing keys and secrets:** never publish.
- **Compiled APK:** do not describe as open-source source code.
- **References to other apps:** do not imply affiliation or permission.
